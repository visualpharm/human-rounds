The AI-native medical platform for public health

AI reads handwritten referral orders from a photo and staff approve them in one click. Patients book online and on WhatsApp, and every approved visit goes to the public health record. Live today in a public hospital in Argentina. The AI interview before the visit is a prototype. No licence fee.

Live demo Documentation GitHub

What it does around each visit

Your existing health record stays the source of truth, and staff confirm every AI proposal.

  1. 01

    Pre-visit interview

    The patient describes the problem in their own words; the AI asks follow-up questions and proposes an urgency for staff to confirm. Prototype.

  2. 02

    Today’s list for each doctor

    Each doctor sees the day’s patients and their referral orders. Live in Pinamar. The AI summary on that list is a prototype.

  3. 03

    One queue for the desk

    Requests arrive with the AI’s reading of the referral order, staff approve or reject them, and freed slots go to the waitlist. Live in Pinamar.

  4. 04

    Each visit reaches the health record

    Approved appointments are written to the national health record and retried on their own while it is down. Live in Pinamar.

Latest updates

  • 14 November 2026

    Identity claims. Signing up with an ID number that already has an account opens a claim the front desk confirms or rejects, and an unverified account sees no clinical data (appointments, calendar, lab results) until it is confirmed.

  • 25 September 2026

    Clinical summary concept. A new homepage section compares today’s note-heavy AI clinical notes with our design direction for a clearer summary — icons, flags, and combined trend charts — a concept built on fictional data, not yet in the product.

  • 31 July 2026

    QR pre-consult interview, prototype. Scan the code at the consulting-room door and the AI interview starts on the patient’s own phone, ready for the doctor before the encounter.

  • 30 July 2026

    Scan-to-register, prototype. A document camera reads an ID and insurance credential, fills the health record, and learns unfamiliar QR formats for the next patient.

  • 29 July 2026

    Resilient EHR handoff. Registration work stays queued locally and retries automatically when the clinical record comes back online.
