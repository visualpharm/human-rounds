The open-source, AI-native medical platform

Humans do the rounds. AI does the rest.

AI interviews patients before the visit, reads handwritten referral orders from a photo, judges urgency, and writes to the health record. The care team walks in prepared. Live today in a real public hospital. Free to run, open to inspect.

Live demo Open source

The encounter is the point.

Human Rounds was rebuilt with artificial intelligence in mind from the start. AI handles the repetitive work before, during and after care while the existing clinical record remains authoritative.

  1. Before the visit

    Encounter and prioritize

    The AI asks what's wrong, follows up, and judges urgency — so the most urgent patients are seen first.

  2. During the visit

    Surface the relevant history

    No more typing through the visit. The dashboard surfaces history, key facts, trends, and charts on sight.

  3. Around the visit

    Coordinate the patient journey

    One system runs registration, booking, arrivals, queues, and insurance claims, start to finish.

  4. After the visit

    Record and automate the outcome

    The visit is summarized automatically, and prescriptions, orders, and test requests are generated with it.

Use cases

Three letters to a cardiologist

Grace needs to see a cardiologist. She doesn't know which health centre has one, or when.

  • 3 letters → the right service, nearest first
  • 2 clicks, no phone call
  • Blood-test order arrives a week ahead
  • One visit instead of two

The doctor walks in prepared

Dr. Nguyen opens today's list before the first patient arrives. Each row shows a triage colour and a one-line summary — no file to dig through.

  • Triage colour + one-line summary, no digging
  • SOAP note & differential ready before he speaks
  • He checks the boxes on orders he agrees with
  • No typing during the visit

A queue that reads itself

Sonia starts her morning with a queue of referral orders — some handwritten, some photos taken in a hurry.

  • AI reads every referral overnight, handwriting included
  • Proposes accept or reject, with the reason attached
  • Rejected patients get the next step, not a dead end
  • No freed slot sits empty
